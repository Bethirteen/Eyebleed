Eyebleed.NET is a digital art project by the artist, B13, designed to evoke internal discomfort through drawings scanned and uploaded from the artist's real-world notebooks. The intended discomfort isn't meant to being a punishment inflicted on the viewer, but more of a form of shared intimacy between viewer and artist.

"Life is brutal"
